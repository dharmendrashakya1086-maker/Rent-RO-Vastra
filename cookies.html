Cookie & Storage Policy

We don't use tracking cookies — here's exactly what this site stores

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. The short answer: do you need a cookie banner?

No cookie-consent banner is strictly required, because this site sets no cookies and runs no cross-site tracking. It stores your data using the browser's localStorage instead — a non-cookie web-storage API that stays on your device. For transparency we still show a small notice the first time you visit, so you always know how the site stores data. This page is that notice written out in full.

2. What the site stores in your browser

The following keys are written to your browser's localStorage on this site:

  • luxe_products — the product catalogue loaded into your browser.
  • luxe_users — accounts registered in this browser (name, email, phone, password hash).
  • luxe_cart / luxe_cart_<email> — your cart (guest and per-account).
  • luxe_wishlist / luxe_wish_<email> — your wishlist (guest and per-account).
  • luxe_orders — orders placed in this browser.
  • luxe_messages — messages/conversations in this browser.
  • luxe_reviews — reviews posted in this browser.
  • luxe_chat_log — your recent chat history with the assistant.
  • luxe_chat_voice — whether voice replies are on.
  • luxe_consent — records that you acknowledged this notice (a tick mark only).

All of these are local to your device. None are sent to us, and none are readable by other websites.

3. Tracking and analytics

The site includes no analytics scripts, no advertising trackers, no pixels and no third-party cookies. Not even first-party cookies are used. There is no way for us, or anyone else, to follow you around the web from this site.

4. Other services that your browser may contact

  • Google Fonts: the site downloads its fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. Your IP address and basic request metadata are sent to Google when the fonts load.
  • Unsplash: demonstration product images load from images.unsplash.com, sending request metadata to Unsplash.
  • Google Gemini API: when you use the chat assistant, your chat message is sent to Google to generate a reply. See the Privacy Policy, section 4.

5. How to control or delete this data

  • Delete account: the Account page offers "Delete Account", which removes the account, cart and wishlist.
  • Clear site data: in your browser's settings, clear cookies and site data for this domain — this erases everything this site stored locally.
  • Withdraw chat consent: simply stop using the chat assistant, or turn off voice replies from the mic icon in the chat.

6. Consent under the DPDP Act / IT Rules

Because the site stores personal data through localStorage (not cookies), we treat your first-visit acknowledgement of this notice as the consent notice required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing the site's data as described above. See our Privacy Policy for your full rights.
