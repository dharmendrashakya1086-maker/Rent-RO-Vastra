The Process

How It Works

Luxury made effortless, from selection to return

01

Browse & Select

Explore our curated collection of occasion wear. Filter by occasion, brand, size, or color to find your perfect look. Every item is inspected and steam-cleaned before every rental.

02

Choose Your Dates

Select your rental period — from a single day to a full week. We deliver 1-2 days before your event so you have time to try everything on and make sure it's perfect.

03

Receive & Try

Your pieces arrive in a luxury garment bag, freshly pressed and ready to wear. Not quite right? We offer free exchanges within 24 hours of delivery.

04

Make Your Statement

Wear your look with confidence. Whether it's a gala, wedding, interview, or night out — own every room you walk into.

05

Return Effortlessly

When you're done, simply place everything back in the provided bag and schedule a pickup. No dry cleaning needed — we handle everything.

06

Rate & Repeat

Share your experience and browse for your next occasion. Rent-RO-Vastra members get early access to new arrivals and exclusive discounts.

FAQ

Common Questions

What if the item doesn't fit?

We offer free exchanges within 24 hours of delivery. Contact us and we'll send a replacement in your size right away.

Do I need to dry clean before returning?

No. We handle all cleaning and maintenance. Just return the item in the provided bag.

What's the damage policy?

Minor wear from normal use is expected. Significant damage, stains or loss remains the renter's responsibility and may incur repair or replacement charges.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 1-2 weeks ahead for best availability. Popular items book fast during wedding and gala season.
