Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear rules on cancellations, refunds, exchanges and damages

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy applies to all rentals booked through Rent-RO-Vastra. It is part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Payment is on delivery

There is no online payment today. You pay by cash or UPI at your doorstep after trying on the outfit and confirming you want to keep the rental. Because no money changes hands online, refunds are issued as store credit for your next rental rather than bank reversals.

2. Cancellation by you

  • 48+ hours before delivery: free cancellation, full value back (as store credit).
  • Within 48 hours of delivery: a 50% charge applies; the remaining value is returned as store credit. This covers the outfit being blocked for other renters and our preparation time.
  • You can cancel from My Rentals on the Account page, or message us on the Messages page.

3. Cancellation by us / unavailability

If we are unable to deliver a confirmed outfit (for example, it is damaged or double-booked), you get a full refund of everything you paid, plus a 10% credit on your next rental. We will contact you on the Messages page as soon as we know.

4. Exchanges

  • Free exchange within 24 hours of delivery if an outfit doesn't fit (S-3XL for men, XS-XXL for women), subject to availability.
  • Bring the garment back in its original condition and packing.
  • Exchanges after 24 hours are handled case by case and may not be possible if the original rental window has started.

5. Returns after delivery

Because these are hygiene-sensitive garments, there are no returns after you have worn an outfit, except where Indian law gives you rights you cannot waive (e.g. under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019). Damaged, stained, altered, washed, or burnt pieces cannot be returned; you will be charged for repair or replacement (see section 6).

6. Damage and loss

  • Minor wear from normal use (light creasing) is expected and free of charge.
  • Significant damage — stains, burns, tears, or any alteration — is the renter's responsibility. We will quote an actual repair charge or, if the piece cannot be repaired, a replacement cost based on the rental price and remaining useful life of the piece.
  • Any insurance coverage held by us does not transfer to you, and it never covers wilful damage. Please treat every piece carefully.

7. Refund method and time

  • Store credit is applied to your account within 48 hours of a valid cancellation.
  • Credit can be used on any future rental and never expires, unless we tell you otherwise at the time.
  • Where a payment has been collected by UPI and law requires a refund to the original payment instrument, we will process it back to your bank within 5–7 working days and confirm on the Messages page.

8. How to request a refund

Send us a message via the Messages page or the Contact page with your order details. We respond Mon–Sat, 10am–9pm IST.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above shows the latest revision. Your booking is governed by the policy in force at the time you place it.
