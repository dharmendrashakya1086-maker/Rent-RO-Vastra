Privacy Policy

How Rent-RO-Vastra collects, uses and protects your information

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rent-RO-Vastra ("we", "our", "us") collects when you use this website (the "Site"), why we collect it, and the rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. Please read it together with our Terms & Conditions and Cookie & Storage Policy.

1. What data we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, and only what is needed to run the service:

  • When you register: your name, email address, phone number and a password (stored as a one-way hash where your browser supports it).
  • When you place a rental order: your name, email, phone, delivery address, district and pincode.
  • When you contact us or send messages: your name, email, phone and the content of your message.
  • When you post a review: your name, rating and review text.
  • When you use the chat assistant: the text and voice messages you send. Messages are passed to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate a reply — see section 4.
  • Usage data automatically sent by your browser: basic request data such as IP address and user agent (see section 5).

2. How your data is stored

This website is browser-based: account, rental history, messages, reviews, cart, wishlist and settings are stored in the browser's localStorage on your own device. This means:

  • Your data is not stored on a Rent-RO-Vastra server.
  • Data is tied to the browser you use; it is not synced across devices or shared with other visitors.
  • Clearing your browser's site data erases it.
  • Because data lives only in your browser, it is not protected by server-side security measures. Do not store sensitive information (e.g. credit card numbers, Aadhaar) on this Site.

3. Why we collect it (purposes)

  • To create and manage your account and log you in (contract performance);
  • To book, confirm and deliver rental orders, and to handle cancellations and refunds (contract performance, Refund Policy);
  • To respond to enquiries and support requests (consent);
  • To show reviews from other renters (publication is with your consent);
  • To operate the chat assistant (consent).

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing, and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking (see Cookie & Storage Policy).

4. AI chat assistant and cross-border processing

When you use the chat assistant, your message (and any voice input you dictate) is sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate a reply. Google may process this content on servers located outside India. By using the chat feature you consent to this transfer. Voice replies are generated by your browser's built-in speech synthesizer and do not leave your device.

5. Third parties who see technical data

  • Google Fonts and Unsplash: the Site loads fonts and demonstration images from these services. Loading them sends your browser's IP address and basic request metadata to those providers.
  • Google (Gemini API): receives chat messages as described above. Your browser may also send microphone audio to a speech-recognition service offered by your browser/vendor; see your browser's privacy policy.

We have no control over these providers' privacy practices. We do not intentionally share any other personal data with them.

6. Retention

Data is kept for as long as it remains in your browser. You can delete your account from the account page. Note that an account deletion removes the account, cart and wishlist, but orders, messages, reviews and chat history remain in your browser wherever they were stored unless you also clear the site's data. We will add a server-side retention and true erasure policy when a backend is introduced.

7. Children

This Site is intended for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data through our services, contact us so we can help you remove it.

8. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information (it is visible to you in the account, orders and messages sections);
  • Correct it (edit your profile from the account page);
  • Erasure / withdraw consent — delete your account, or clear the Site's data from your browser; and
  • Complain — you may raise a complaint with us (below) or with the Data Protection Board of India (under s. 33 of the DPDP Act, 2023).
